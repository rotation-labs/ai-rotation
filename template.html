How to read this

Axes. Right of center means the group is outperforming its benchmark's recent trend (RS-Ratio above 100); above center means that relative strength is accelerating (RS-Momentum above 100). The two raw measures have very different spreads, so each axis is divided by its own dispersion across what is on screen and labelled in standard deviations: +1 means one σ from the crossover, and one σ is the same visual distance on both axes. The window is square and centered, so a rotation traces a circle rather than an ellipse. Groups tend to rotate clockwise: Improving → Leading → Weakening → Lagging.

Levels. Full stack compares the 11 layers of the AI trade against SPY. Open a layer to see its subsectors (semis layers are measured against SMH, so you see rotation within semis rather than the semis beta), then open a subsector to see its stocks.

Lists. Each quadrant is sorted strongest first. The arrow is the direction of the last step, the small number is time spent in that quadrant, NEW marks a crossing in the last two bars, and the percentage is relative performance over the tail.

Method. Baskets are equal-weight and rebalanced daily. A subsector equal-weights its stocks; a layer equal-weights its distinct stocks directly, so subsector size does not change a stock's influence and a name listed in two subsectors of one layer is still counted once. RS = price ÷ benchmark. RS-Ratio = 100 × smoothed RS ÷ its 10-week (21-day) average; RS-Momentum = 100 × the smoothed one-bar change in RS-Ratio. This approximates the proprietary JdK RRG, so levels differ from StockCharts but quadrants and rotation behave the same way. AVGO, MRVL and TSEM each sit in two different layers, so they appear as members of two separate baskets; AMD sits in two Compute subsectors but counts once toward the Compute layer.
