# Bundled fonts

Geist and Geist Mono, by Vercel, licensed under the SIL Open Font License 1.1.

Upstream and full licence text: https://github.com/vercel/geist-font

These are the `latin` subset variable builds as served by Google Fonts. They are
vendored here so the published page makes no third-party request: loading them
from fonts.googleapis.com disclosed every visitor of this site to Google.
